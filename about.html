About This Project

Welcome to the Lebanese Language Reference! This site is designed to be a simple, community-driven dictionary for learning Lebanese Arabic words and phrases.

The goal is to make it easy for anyone to look up words and even contribute new ones. If you know a word that's missing, please feel free to submit a Pull Request on GitHub!
